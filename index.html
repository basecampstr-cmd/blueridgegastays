Lake Days

Three mountain lakes

Clear, cool, and ringed by national forest — the Blue Ridge lakes are made for slow boat days, paddleboards, and swimming holes.

~3,290 acres · TVA

Lake Blue Ridge

The closest lake to town — emerald-clear water, mostly undeveloped national-forest shoreline, and a public marina. Great for pontoons, kayaks, and trout right below the dam.

~4,180 acres · Blairsville

Lake Nottely

About 40 minutes east near Blairsville — a quieter, sprawling lake known for bass, walleye, and crappie, with Poteete Creek Park and easy public ramps.

~7,050 acres · Hiawassee

Lake Chatuge

Straddling the GA/NC line near Hiawassee and Young Harris — postcard mountain backdrops, big open water, and our River Run cabins right nearby.

What's on the Water

Blue Ridge is trout country

This isn't a bass-lake scene — it's one of the Southeast's premier fly-fishing destinations. The Toccoa River tailwater below Blue Ridge Dam runs cold and clear year-round, holding wild and stocked rainbows and browns. Always check flows before you wade.

Tailwater

Toccoa River (Tailwater)

Below Blue Ridge Dam: cold, dam-controlled water and the area's signature trout float. Flows swing with TVA generation — check the live cfs above before heading out.

Headwaters & Creeks

The upper Toccoa, Noontootla Creek, Rock Creek, and Cooper Creek hold wild trout in the Aska Adventure Area and Chattahoochee forest — small water, big scenery.
